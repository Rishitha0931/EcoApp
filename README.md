#  EcoApp

> **Making Environmental Learning Fun and Impactful Through Technology**

EcoApp is a **Flutter + Firebase** powered gamified environmental learning platform that transforms sustainability education into an engaging digital experience through eco-challenges, quizzes, rewards, and role-based learning.

Built as a **Hackathon Project**, EcoApp encourages students to develop eco-friendly habits while enabling teachers and administrators to monitor participation and community impact through a centralized platform.

---

#  About The Project

Traditional environmental education often focuses on theory, leaving students with limited opportunities to practice sustainable habits in their daily lives.

EcoApp bridges this gap by providing a digital platform where students actively participate in environmental activities, complete quizzes, earn rewards, and compete through gamified challenges. Teachers create educational content and verify activities, while administrators oversee the entire platform.

By combining **education**, **technology**, and **gamification**, EcoApp makes environmental learning practical, engaging, and impactful.

---

#  Problem Statement

Many students learn about environmental sustainability only through textbooks, resulting in low engagement and minimal real-world participation.

There is a need for an interactive platform that motivates students to actively practice environmentally responsible behaviors while making learning enjoyable and measurable.

EcoApp addresses this challenge through gamification, role-based learning, and real-world eco activities.
